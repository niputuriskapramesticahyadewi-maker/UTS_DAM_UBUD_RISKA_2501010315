Member Hotels & Resorts

  1. home
  2. member hotels & resorts

The Bali Hotels Association is a distinguished organization representing star-rated hotels and resorts in Bali.

As a leading voice for the hospitality industry, it advocates for the rights and interests of its members at local, regional, and national levels. The association fosters industry leadership, champions best practices in hotel management, and ensures clear and effective communication with its members, stakeholders, and the wider community.

Compliance with Bali Governor Circular SE No. 7 Tahun 2025
All Bali Hotel Association (BHA) members are fully licensed accommodation providers.
In accordance with Bali Governor Circular SE No. 7 Tahun 2025 on Regulations for Foreign Tourists, all foreign visitors are required to stay in legally compliant, licensed accommodations.

BHA remains committed to supporting sustainable and responsible tourism in Bali by ensuring that all member establishments adhere to the latest legal and regulatory standards. Bali Hotels Association - Hospitality with Purpose!
